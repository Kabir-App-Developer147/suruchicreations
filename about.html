The Story

Art That Breathes

Gurmit Lamba is a self-taught artist whose love for colour and texture led her to the world of resin art. Working from her studio in India, she creates pieces that capture movement, depth, and light in a way few other mediums can.

Her work spans resin pours, abstract paintings, decorative objects, and bespoke commissions. Each piece is made entirely by hand — mixed, poured, and finished with meticulous care over several days.

Gurmit believes that art should feel alive. Her resin pieces shift in colour as light changes throughout the day, making each one a living companion in your space.

The Process

How a Piece is Born

01

Concept & Colour

Each piece begins with a colour story — inspired by nature, memory, or feeling. Pigments are chosen and mixed before a single drop of resin is poured.

02

The Pour

Resin is mixed and poured in layers. Each layer must cure before the next — a process of patience that can span several days for complex pieces.

03

Finishing

Once fully cured, pieces are sanded, polished, and sealed to a glass-like finish. Details are added and the work is inspected before being photographed.

04

Finding Its Home

Every piece is packed carefully and shipped — ready to become part of your space.
